# Upstox — Beginner Investing Experience Product Case Study

> **Helping beginners understand before they decide.**

A product case study focused on improving the **first-investment experience for beginner investors** by reducing the effort required to interpret existing investment information.

The project combines **primary user research, data analysis, product auditing, product thinking, UX exploration, MVP definition, and validation planning** to identify an opportunity within the investing journey.

# 1. The Problem

Beginner investors have access to a large amount of information:

- Stock prices
- Price trends
- Financial performance
- Valuation
- Company information
- Market news
- Events
- Competitors
- Risk-related information

However, having access to information does not necessarily mean users understand what it means.

The core problem identified through this project was:

> **Beginners can access investment information, but often struggle to turn it into a clear understanding before deciding.**

     ↓
